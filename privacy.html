Lab Exam Console

Privacy policy

For the Software Engineering lab exam at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences.

What the exam uses

  • Google sign-in. When a student signs in with their college Google account, the page reads only their name and email address from Google. It asks Google for nothing else and cannot read their mail, Drive, or any other data.
  • What the student enters: register number, question number, UML diagrams, Python code, and program output.
  • Exam conduct events, such as leaving fullscreen or switching windows, with their times.

Where it is kept

  • All of it stays in the student's own browser on the lab computer while the exam runs, so the exam can resume after a reload.
  • At the end, the browser makes one PDF report that the student downloads. The student hands that file in, for example through a Google Form set up by the teacher.
  • The exam site has no server and no database. It does not send the student's answers, name, or email anywhere.

Other services the page loads

  • Google sign-in (accounts.google.com), under Google's own privacy policy.
  • The diagrams.net editor (embed.diagrams.net), which draws the diagrams inside the page. The diagrams stay in the browser.

The page uses no cookies of its own, no analytics, and no advertising.

Removing the data

Clearing the browser's site data for this page removes everything the exam stored on that computer. Submitted reports are kept by the teacher as course records.

Contact

Questions about this exam or this policy: contact your course teacher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.
